Whetstone makes Brief

Brief: three briefs a day, a quiz, and a leaderboard.

The day, read closely. Brief is a news app for iPhone that gives you three short briefs on the day’s news, then a quiz to see what stuck.

  1. Read today’s three briefs. They’re written with AI from the day’s reporting, and every idea links to the sources it came from.
  2. Take the quiz. Every point is graded on the server.
  3. Climb the week’s board. It resets every Monday.

Free keeps the daily three briefs, the quiz, the leaderboard and joining squads. Brief Pro adds Debate, Deep Dive, Past editions and starting your own squad.
